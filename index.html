FAQ

Questions, answered

Do you use PBNs or link farms?

Never. Every placement is on a real publication with genuine traffic and an editorial team. If a site looks like a network, it doesn’t make the list.

How soon will I see results?

It depends on competition and where your site starts. Most placements go live within a few weeks, and ranking movement usually follows over the next one to three months. I track and report progress, but I don’t promise guaranteed rankings.

Do you work with agencies?

Yes. Custom and white-label plans are available, agencies can resell my services under their own brand, and your client details stay confidential.

What do you need from me to get started?

Your website, target pages and keywords, preferred niches or publications, and your budget range. That’s enough for me to send back a clear link-building plan.
