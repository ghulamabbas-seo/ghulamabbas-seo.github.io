Featured case study · Medical niche · Dubai, UAE

Ranking #2 on Page 1 for “Endolift in Dubai”

Many SEOs chase vanity metrics like Domain Rating. What drives business is rankings, targeted traffic and conversions. Here is how premium Digital PR helped a client reach the top of Google.

The Challenge

Ranking for high-intent, commercially competitive keywords in the premium UAE healthcare market, specifically the high-value treatment keyword “Endolift in Dubai”.

The Strategy

Instead of risking penalties with PBNs or spammy directories, I secured a contextual placement on Khaleej Times, one of the UAE’s biggest news publications.

  • Clean, optimized anchor text around “Endolift in Dubai”
  • Enfield Royal Clinic positioned as a top recommended choice
  • Authority and local relevance from a trusted news source

The Result

#2

organic position on Page 1 of Google for the competitive keyword “Endolift in Dubai”.

A strong ranking foundation built on clean links.

Beyond Khaleej Times, I run outreach campaigns on major media such as Gulf News, USA Today, TechRadar and more, through publisher relationships and Digital PR / Connectively (HARO) outreach.
