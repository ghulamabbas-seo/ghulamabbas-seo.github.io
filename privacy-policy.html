Privacy Policy

How I collect, use and protect your information. Last updated: November 24, 2026.

1. Who I am

This website is run by Ghulam Abbas, an independent SEO Outreach & Digital PR Specialist based in Islamabad, Pakistan (“I”, “me”). This policy explains what personal information I collect and how I use and protect it.

2. Information I collect

  • Contact details — name, email and message when you contact me by form, email, WhatsApp or LinkedIn.
  • Project details — website, target pages, keywords, niches and budget when you request a quote.
  • Billing details — what is needed to invoice and receive payment. I do not store card numbers.
  • Technical data — basic data collected by the website host, such as browser type and pages visited.

3. How I use it

  • To reply to enquiries and send proposals.
  • To plan and deliver outreach, guest posting and digital PR campaigns.
  • To share live links and reports.
  • To send invoices and keep business records.

I never sell your personal information or send marketing emails you didn’t ask for.

4. When I share it

  • Publishers — only what a placement needs, such as your URL, anchor text and article.
  • Service providers — hosting (GitHub Pages), the contact-form service FormSubmit, and email and messaging tools.
  • Legal reasons — if required by law.

5. Cookies

The website host may use basic cookies for security and statistics. You can block them in your browser and the site will still work.

6. How long I keep data

Enquiries are kept as long as needed to follow up; client and billing records as long as business and tax rules require. After that they are deleted.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time. If you’re in the UK, EU or another region with data-protection laws, you may have extra rights. Email abbasseoagency@gmail.com.

8. Security

I use reasonable safeguards such as secure, password-protected accounts, but no online system is 100% secure.

9. Third-party sites

This site links to publisher articles, LinkedIn and WhatsApp, which have their own privacy policies.
