1. Who I am

This website is operated by Ghulam Abbas, an independent SEO Outreach & Digital PR Specialist based in Gulberg Green Executive Block, Islamabad, Pakistan (“I”, “me”, “my”). This policy explains what personal information I collect when you visit this website or work with me, and how I use and protect it.

2. Information I collect

I only collect information that you choose to share or that is needed to run this website:

  • Contact details — your name, email address and message when you use the contact form, email me or message me on WhatsApp or LinkedIn.
  • Project details — your website URL, target pages, keywords, preferred publications, niche and budget when you request a quote or start a campaign.
  • Billing details — information needed to send invoices and receive payment. I do not store full card numbers.
  • Technical data — basic data collected automatically by the website host, such as IP address, browser type and pages visited.

3. How I use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and send you a proposal or quote.
  • To plan and deliver SEO outreach, guest posting and digital PR campaigns.
  • To share live links, reports and campaign updates with you.
  • To send invoices and keep business and tax records.
  • To keep this website secure and understand how it is used.

I do not sell your personal information, and I do not send marketing emails unless you ask to receive them.

4. When I share information

I share information only when it is needed to deliver your campaign or run my business:

  • Publishers and media partners — I share only what is needed for a placement, such as your website URL, anchor text and article content.
  • Service providers — such as website hosting (GitHub Pages), the contact-form service FormSubmit (which delivers form messages to my email), email and messaging services that process data on my behalf.
  • Legal reasons — if required by law or to protect my rights.

5. Cookies

This website does not set its own tracking cookies. The hosting platform and embedded services (such as Google Fonts) may process basic technical data for security and performance. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work.

6. How long I keep data

I keep enquiry messages for as long as needed to respond and follow up, and client project and billing records for as long as required for business and tax purposes. After that, the data is deleted or anonymised.

7. Your rights

You can ask me at any time to:

  • See the personal information I hold about you.
  • Correct information that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Delete your information, where I don’t need to keep it for legal reasons.
  • Stop using your information for a particular purpose.

If you are in the UK, EU or another region with data-protection laws, you may have additional rights under those laws. To make a request, email abbasseoagency@gmail.com.

8. Security

I take reasonable steps to protect your information, such as using secure, password-protected accounts. No method of sending or storing data online is 100% secure, so I cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Third-party websites

This website links to articles on publisher websites, LinkedIn and WhatsApp. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and I am not responsible for how they handle your data.

10. Children’s privacy

My services are for businesses and adults. I do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

11. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page with its “Last updated” date.
