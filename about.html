My story

From marketing to outreach that moves rankings

I started in digital marketing, leading campaigns as a Senior Marketing Manager at Zaji Digital Agency. Working closely with clients taught me one thing again and again: brands that earned links from trusted publications grew faster — and safer — than those chasing shortcuts.

So I went all-in on SEO outreach and digital PR. Today I work with agencies and brands across the US, UK, UAE and Pakistan, including SEO Agency London, Bright Ideas Communications in Dubai and Link Building Services in the US.

In 2025 I founded Market Business Magazine, a multi-niche digital publication. That means I understand outreach from both sides of the inbox — as the person pitching, and as the publisher deciding what gets published.
