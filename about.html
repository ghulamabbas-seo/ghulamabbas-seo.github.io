My story

From marketing to outreach that moves rankings

I started my career in digital marketing, leading campaigns as a Senior Marketing Manager at Zaji Digital Agency. Working closely with clients showed me one thing again and again: brands that earned links from trusted publications grew faster and more safely than those chasing shortcuts.

That’s why I focused on SEO outreach and digital PR. Today I build relationships with publishers and media platforms across international markets, and I work with agencies and brands in the US, UK, UAE and Pakistan — including SEO Agency London, Bright Ideas Communications in Dubai and Link Building Services in the US.

One of my proudest results was helping Enfield Royal Clinic rank #2 on Page 1 of Google for “Endolift in Dubai” through a placement on Khaleej Times. In 2025 I also founded Market Business Magazine, a multi-niche digital publication — so I understand outreach from both sides: as the person pitching, and as the publisher receiving the pitch.
